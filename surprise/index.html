Dear Saskia,
We made a long trip to here
To bring you some skiing gear.
We sailed by boat to make it quicker,
And this fun fact should make you bitter,
Because you love do sailing trips
And bravely dive in ocean dips.

Instead, you live in god-knows-where
Far-far away from seaside air.
The opportunities in Gelselaar are phony,
So Saskia can here only ride circles on a pony.
Or play Beethoven on a piano in a rainy day
Instead of mooring a yacht in a tropical bay.

But let’s be on the positive side,
At least you can go again for a skiing ride.
With the children and husband you will live in a chalet
(Oh sorry, he is not a husband, you are not married yet).

But it’s nice anyway of five of you going together,
To enjoy the mountains and the winter weather.
But the size of your family will be soon more wild
Since Linde wants to get in her sixteen a child.
So you will ruin much more neighbors’ holidays,
By the children who are crying all the days.

We brought you a pair of socks of a jungle theme,
Since you have an exotic trips dream.
You may probably ask: where is a pun?
We gifted Jelmer three of such pairs, not only one.
The same brand and purpose, even smaller size,
Somehow your socks had significantly higher price.
But there is a practical part, we have a forecast:
Jelmer’s socks will get smelly much more fast.

Anyway, good luck with the socks.
Ski conscious a bit.
Best regards,
Sinterklaas and Piet
